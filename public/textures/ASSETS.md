# Generated countryside textures

Created with the built-in image generation tool, using the imagegen skill. Both selected outputs were copied into this project without image editing. The foliage and grass are generated; Earth maps are credited below.

## Oak foliage

Saved path: `public/textures/oak-foliage.png`
Transparent background: true. Used on overlapping foliage cards in the 3D trees.

Final prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: alpha-cutout foliage texture for a realistic 3D countryside driving game. Create ONE natural, irregular, roughly round cluster of dense English oak and beech leaves on tiny branching twigs, isolated on a genuinely transparent background. Front-facing orthographic botanical photograph, fills 90% of a square canvas. Hundreds of individually detailed small leaves, muted natural summer olive green, some dark interior leaves, subtle pale veins and slightly varied leaf orientations, tiny gaps between leaves with true transparency. Cluster should be volumetric and densely layered in the middle with irregular wispy natural edges. Soft neutral overcast illumination, NO strong baked shadow, no trunk, no whole tree silhouette, no pot, no ground, no sky, no text, no border. This texture will be used on overlapping small foliage cards in a 3D tree, not as a complete tree image. High-frequency realistic leaf detail; avoid illustration or cartoon.

## Meadow grass

Saved path: `public/textures/meadow-grass.png`
Transparent background: false. Used as a repeating ground albedo texture.

Final prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless repeating ground albedo texture for a realistic 3D English countryside driving game. A straight-down orthographic photograph of a two-metre-square patch of dense short meadow grass, naturally varied muted olive and fresh green fine blades, tiny occasional dry straw, slightly uneven mottled growth with no bare soil or flowers. Neutral diffuse overcast lighting, uniform illumination with no directional shadows, no highlights baked in. Seamlessly tileable on all four edges, no perspective, no horizon, no objects, no text, no border. Small fine natural details, realistic restrained colour, not neon and not a lawn carpet pattern. Square high-resolution texture, fill the entire image edge to edge.
